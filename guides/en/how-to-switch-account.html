How to switch accounts when limited? Multi-account management guide

When your main account's quota runs out, switching to a backup account is a common workaround — but do it safely to avoid triggering platform security.

Steps to switch

  1. Click your avatar (bottom-left) on the current account
  2. Choose 'Sign out'
  3. Clear browser cache (optional, prevents session mix-up)
  4. Log back in with your backup account's email and password
  5. Confirm login and continue using

Multi-account management tips

  • Use different emails for each account — no cross-linking
  • Set unique passwords, use a password manager
  • Track each account's reset time and rotate usage
  • Don't log into multiple accounts in the same browser

Notes (avoid flags)

Frequent account switching may trigger security detection:

  • Don't sign in/out repeatedly in a short time
  • Avoid logging into multiple accounts from the same IP simultaneously
  • Don't use scripts to batch-create accounts
  • Follow the ToS — don't share accounts

FAQ

Will I lose chat history when switching?

Yes. Chat history is per-account. You can't see other accounts' history after switching. Export important content beforehand.

Can I be logged in on phone and desktop simultaneously?

Yes, most products support multi-device login for the same account, but they share one quota.
