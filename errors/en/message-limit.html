Message limit reached — You've hit your message quota

Message limits are the core mechanism providers use to control per-user call frequency. Once reached, some features are locked until the next reset cycle.

Message limit reached. Please wait for the reset.
通用型 AI 助手限额提示

Why you hit the limit

This appears when you send many messages in a short time, or when your daily total reaches your account tier's cap. It's a normal anti-abuse and stability measure.

How reset cycles work

Most products reset on a 'calendar day' (UTC or a specific timezone midnight), while some use a 'rolling 24-hour' window from your last use. Check account settings or official docs for specifics.

How to avoid hitting limits often

  • Combine multiple questions into one message
  • Use longer context windows to reduce round-trips
  • Use during off-peak hours
  • Track official reset times and plan ahead

FAQ

What still works when limited?

Basic chat is usually locked, but history browsing and file management may still work. API calls are not affected by web limits.
